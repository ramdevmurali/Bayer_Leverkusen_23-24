# Bayer Leverkusen 2023/24 Tactical Analysis

This repository hosts a standalone scouting and tactical analysis report on Bayer Leverkusen's 2023/24 season, with a particular focus on Florian Wirtz, Granit Xhaka, team progression patterns, and defensive structure.

The full report is available here: [Bayer Leverkusen 23-24 .pdf](./Bayer%20Leverkusen%2023-24%20.pdf)

## Overview

Written by **Ramdev Murali** in **September 2024**, this report uses match-event data and visual analysis to examine how Bayer Leverkusen combined attacking creativity, midfield control, and defensive organization during one of the club's strongest modern seasons.

Rather than serving as a code repository, this repo is intentionally kept minimal and publication-focused. The PDF is the primary deliverable.

## What the Report Covers

- Late-goal scoring patterns and end-of-match resilience
- Expected goals versus actual goals across the season
- Passing network structure and possession flow
- Final-third progression, including the Xhaka-to-Wirtz connection
- Pressing intensity through PPDA
- Team defensive action heatmaps and top defensive contributors
- Pass accuracy and volume across the squad
- Wirtz's chance creation, carries, and attacking heatmaps
- Xhaka's spatial influence in buildup and midfield control
- Defensive positioning and structural flexibility

## Key Takeaways

- **Florian Wirtz** emerges as the team's creative hub in advanced areas, combining key passes, assists, carries, and final-third involvement.
- **Granit Xhaka** functions as the central organizer, driving circulation, progression, and tempo from deeper midfield zones.
- Bayer Leverkusen's attack is built on both **controlled progression** and **high-quality chance creation**, with goals often matching or exceeding expected output.
- The team shows a strong collective defensive identity, with pressing and defensive actions distributed across midfielders, defenders, and even attacking players.
- Spatial and passing-network analysis suggest a side that is both **tactically flexible** and **structurally coherent**, especially in transitions between buildup and attack.
